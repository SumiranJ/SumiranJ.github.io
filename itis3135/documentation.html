Quantum Focus Interval Timer

Technical Specifications

  • Battery: 3.7V Rechargable Lithium-Polymer
  • Alert Modes: Silent Vibration / Audio Beep (high/low)
  • Mounting: Integrated N52 Neodymium Magnet

Initial Setup & Charging

  1. Unbox: Remove the protective film from the high-contrast LCD screen.
  2. Charge: Plug the provided USB-C cable into the side port.
  3. Indicator: A solid red LED means charging. Solid green means fully charged.
  4. Power: Press and hold the Center Start/Stop button for 3 seconds.

Configuring the Pace Intervals

To setup a 25 minute focus sprint followed by a 5-minute movement break:

  1. Press the MODE button until INT appears on the screen.
  2. Press MIN to set the focus interval to 25:00.
  3. Press MODE once to switch to the break interval setting.
  4. Press MIN to set the break interval to 05:00.
  5. Toggle the side switch to VIBE for silent desk alerts.
  6. Press START. The timer will now loop between these intervals automatically.
⚠️ WARNING: Do not submerge the timer in water. Keep away from high-temperature environments exceeding 140°F (60°C).

Ergonomic Agility Jump Rope

Technical Specifications

  • Cable Material: 3mm PVC Coated Braided Steel
  • Bearing System: 360° High-Speed Ball Bearings
  • Handle Weight: 0.2 lbs (Removable weights inside)

Custom Sizing Instructions

For optimal performance and safety, size the cable to your height before the first use:

  1. Position: Stand on the exact center of the rope with one foot.
  2. Measure: Pull the handles straight up along your torso.
  3. Target: The top of the handles should align exactly with your armpits.
  4. Adjust: Unscrew the handle caps. Loosen the thumb screw mechanism on the cable.
  5. Cut: Slide the cable to your desired length. Use wire cutters to trim the excess steel cable.
  6. Lock: Tighten the thumb screw securely. Re-attach the rubber end cap.
⚠️ SAFETY CHECK: Always inspect the thumb screws before high-speed use. Loose screws can cause the cable to detach mid-sprint.

Catalyst Desktop Zen Sandbox

Material Composition

  • Tray: Solid American Walnut (Sealed)
  • Sand: 100% Calcite White Micro-Sand (Non-Toxic)

Assembly & Maintenance

  1. Placement: Place the walnut tray on a completely level desk surface.
  2. Pour: Slowly empty the sand packet into the center of the tray.
  3. Level: Use the flat side of the miniature wooden rake to distribute the sand evenly.
  4. Sanitization: To clean the sand, gently sift it using a fine-mesh strainer.
  5. Wood Care: Wipe the walnut frame with a dry microfiber cloth. Do not use chemical cleaners.
