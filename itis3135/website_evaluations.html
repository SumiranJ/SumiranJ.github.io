Website Evaluations

Niner Times - Well Designed

  • Contrast:

    The contrast is well defined on this webpage, with black text over a light background and white text over a dark green background in the navigation bar. The images that contain text that link to news articles are also well designed, with white text on a dark shadow background, and it makes it easier to associate the image with the news story that was linked.

  • Repetition:

    The webpage maintains repetition in this webpage by forming a grid layout and noting each news article with the category (News, Sports, etc.), the title and description. When we click on the links located in the menu bar, it has a list of articles that are associated with a specific topic, image, and title. This format is maintained on all sublinks located in the menu bar.

  • Alignment:

    The webpage is aligned by using a grid layout that sorts the news articles and associated images. The home page has a section to the right side that involves email subscription, recently released paper, and their social media site. This section is beneficial for the user to focus on the news articles, and not become distracted by these elements. The webpage also contains an orderly footer alignment with the necessary hyperlinks.

  • Proximity:

    Proximity is enforced throughout the webpage by grouping news articles into a subtopic section, and using horizontal line breaks for the user to view the various subtopics and its articles. This lets the users quickly recognize the information and find the article they want to read. When I scroll down, the menu bar shows up on the top. This is helpful for me to quickly look through the categories instead of going back to the top.

Tag Team Signs - Poorly Designed

  • Contrast:

    This website contains white & black text on a bright red background. It would be nice if the background color tone was desaturated.

  • Repetition:

    The webpage uses a set of font colors and styles, but it is not consistently applied throughout the page. For example, the “Our Work” section has a white text paragraph, while the section after it, “Our Mission”, has a black text paragraph. The “Who We Are” section has a background image and a red text unordered list, which feels a little hard to read, and this indicates inconsistent design.

  • Alignment:

    The navigation bar does not have a hyperlink to go to the “Our Mission” section, compared to the other sections that do have hyperlinks associated with them. The grid layout simply stacks the elements onto each other. These elements are recognizable, but they could be improved by sorting out sections that do not need to fill the full viewport width, like the “Our Services” section.

  • Proximity:

    The separate sections’ texts are placed together, but there is a section with images that wasn’t specifically noted in the menu bar. This could be improved by naming a separate section for this and grouping these images in that section.
